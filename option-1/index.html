About the studio

Beautiful homes, on any budget.

VM Renovations was founded to make beautiful homes and commercial spaces available on any budget. Technical excellence and expert craftsmanship, from start to finish.

Doors opened in 2021. The team brings over a decade of combined industry experience, and a love of one-of-a-kind, elevated spaces in southeast London.
