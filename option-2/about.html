About us

Young company. A precise eye.

Founded to make beautiful homes and commercial spaces available on any budget. One-of-a-kind, elevated spaces in southeast London.

VM Renovations was founded on the principle of making beautiful homes and commercial spaces available on any budget. The work is technical excellence and expert craftsmanship on every site, from start to finish, so a client’s needs are met and their hopes for the space are realised.

Doors opened in 2021. The background is the London construction industry, site and project management, and a roster of London-based contractors who treat quality as the point. The pleasure is working in southeast London, Dulwich among the neighbourhoods, on spaces that belong to the people who live in them.

Home renovation asks for a precise eye. With over a decade of combined industry experience and careful planning, the team elevates a home with attention to detail, inside the budget that was agreed.
